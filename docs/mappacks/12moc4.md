12 Maps Of Christmas 4 is a winter themed platformer based on SMB and yet another obvious sequel to [12 Maps Of Christmas 3](https://greatbritdan.github.io/archives/mappacks/12moc3/) released for Alesan99's Entities, Originally released on the 24th of December, 2021. It features 12 levels. The mappack has a lot of cool concept and several really interesting bosses.

## Credits
