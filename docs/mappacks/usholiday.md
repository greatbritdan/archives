US Holiday Edition is a small update to the original mappack [US](https://greatbritdan.github.io/archives/mappacks/us/), it was released for Alesan99's Entities on the 22nd of December, 2022. It features a winter aesphetic as well as a brand new map and powerup, as well as several improvements including an item reserve.

## Credits

Several enemies and mechanics (HIM, Improved slingshot, switches, buff him, etc) was made by [WilliamFrog](https://forum.stabyourself.net/memberlist.php?mode=viewprofile&u=4261).
