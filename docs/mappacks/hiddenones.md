(The) Hidden Ones is a unique short, 3 world mappack and release for Alesan99's Entities on the 22nd of June 2020. It features a grassy, desert and apocalyptic world with 2 custom bosses. It was one of if not the first mappack to be released for the then much anticipated V13. Overall the mappack has ages very poorly and is no where near the quality of it's seaquel mappack Lies Awaken.

The original version featured a working world map which also contained additonal content like bonus minigames but was removed due to issues and the functionalliy was lost to time.
