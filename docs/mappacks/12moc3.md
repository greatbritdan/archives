12 Maps Of Christmas 3 is a winter themed platformer based on SMB and another obvious sequel to [12 Maps Of Christmas 2](https://greatbritdan.github.io/archives/mappacks/12moc2/) released for Alesan99's Entities, Originally released on the 23rd of December, 2020. It features 12 levels with 4 additional bonus levels with 1 being locked behind getting 36 purple coins. The mappack is fairly lackluster and despite having some unique ideas it's fairly boring. 

## Interesting Facts

- This mappack was blaintantly stolen and rerelased as "Xmas Scary 3" which featured edited levels and very load jumpscares, since the release of my video mocking it the original has been taken down.
- This mappack features many easter eggs, mainly little pixel representations of emojis from the unoffical Stabyourself.net discord server.
- in 2-4, entering one of the last green pipes might lead you to something special.
