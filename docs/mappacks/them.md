THEM is the second entry in the HIM tetrolagy, it was released for Alesan99's Entities on the 28th of January, 2021. The goal is once again to collect 10 mushrooms before the cursed goomba or his son "mini him" catches you, it features 6 different modes including a rematch which uses the map from the first mappack.
